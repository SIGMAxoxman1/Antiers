How tiers work

Each mode has five tiers and every tier has three levels: High (HT), Mid (MT) and Low (LT). Tier 1 is the top. Your overall score is the sum of the points from all your modes.

1Join the Discord and pick the mode you want to be tested in.
2Wait for a tester in the queue and fight them in a set of matches.
3Your result is posted and your tier appears on this board.
